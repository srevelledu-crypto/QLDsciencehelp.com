    Disclaimer and privacy

    An independent site

    QLDsciencehelp.com is an independent revision site. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the Queensland Curriculum and Assessment Authority (QCAA) or any school.

    About the content

    The revision pages are my own work, written to support students studying the QCE Chemistry and Physics syllabuses. I take care to keep them accurate, but they may contain errors or fall out of date when the syllabus changes.

    Always check what you learn here against the current official syllabus and with your teacher, who knows your course and assessment best. These pages are for revision only and aren’t a substitute for your classes.

    If you find a mistake, please let me know via the Contact page.

    Privacy

    • No accounts. You never sign up or log in, and there are no forms that send information anywhere.
    • No cookies. This site doesn’t set cookies.
    • Your progress stays on your device. The revision pages save the steps you’ve visited and your answers in your browser’s own storage (localStorage). The site also remembers the last topic you opened. None of this is sent to me or anyone else. You can clear it at any time from the About page or by clearing your browser’s site data.
    • Anonymous visitor statistics. To see how many people use the site, I may use Cloudflare Web Analytics, which is cookie-free and doesn’t track you across sites or collect personal information.
    • Fonts on topic pages. The site’s own pages use fonts stored on this site. The topic (revision) pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser may connect to Google’s servers when you open them.
