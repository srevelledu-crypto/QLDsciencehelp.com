    What each revision page contains

    Each page covers one subtopic from the QCE Chemistry or Physics syllabus and walks you through five steps:

    1. RecallBring back the key ideas from memory before you look anything up.
    2. Worked examplesFollow problems solved step by step, with the reasoning shown.
    3. ConsolidatePractise the ideas with short activities and check your answers.
    4. Exam questionsTry exam-style questions, then compare your answer with the solution.
    5. Wrap-upPull it together and note anything you need to revisit.

    Pages are interactive: you can reveal answers, check your work and come back later — your answers and the steps you’ve visited are saved in your browser on this device.

    Your privacy

    • No accounts, no sign-ups and no cookies.
    • Your progress is stored only in your own browser (localStorage). It never leaves your device, and I can’t see it.
    • The site counts visits with anonymous, cookie-free statistics that don’t identify you.

    The full details are on the Disclaimer & privacy page.

    Reset my progress

    This clears the steps you’ve visited, the answers saved on the revision pages and your “continue where you left off” topic — on this device only. Other devices and browsers aren’t affected.
